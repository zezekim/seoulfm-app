SeoulFM is K-pop radio, live and free, since 2009. Twelve stations play around the clock, every song comes with its lyrics, and you choose what plays next.

TWELVE STATIONS, ONE TAP AWAY
Pop! is the original: today's K-pop, shaped by listener requests. Fresh plays releases from the last 60 days. Marathon gives one group a full hour. Then Dance, Indie, HipHop, R&B, Ballad, OST, the 2010s and the Classics. HIFI streams in lossless FLAC, as the studio heard it.

LYRICS THAT FOLLOW THE SONG
Synced lyrics scroll with the music, line by line, on a background drawn from the album cover. Share a line as a card to your stories in a tap.

YOU CHOOSE WHAT PLAYS NEXT
Search the library, which grows every day, and request a song. When it plays, everyone listening hears it, along with your dedication. No account needed.

MADE FOR LISTENING
• Full-screen album art and a player that takes on the cover's colours
• Up Next, recently played, and the week's charts
• Android Auto: pick a station on the car screen, skip with the steering wheel
• Lock screen and notification controls
• Plays in the background, with headset and Bluetooth controls
• A sleep timer that fades out gently
• Choose your streaming quality, or leave it on Auto
• In 20 languages

SeoulFM is free. If you'd like to keep it on air, you can leave a tip or become a monthly supporter in the app. It's entirely optional and everything stays free either way.
